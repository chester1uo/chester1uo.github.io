AddMart reconstructs implied volatility surfaces from sparse option quotes using an exponential additive martingale representation. Its architecture encodes static no-arbitrage constraints through non-negative increment densities, allowing risk-neutral distributions and implied volatilities to be recovered without relying on penalty terms to enforce validity. Evaluations across ten real-option datasets show competitive reconstruction performance with negligible static arbitrage, alongside estimates of value at risk, conditional value at risk, and crash probabilities.
